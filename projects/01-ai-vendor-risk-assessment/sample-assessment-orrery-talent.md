## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 · Customer data used for model training by default (High)
Orrery's standard terms permit use of "customer content" to improve the service, which its data processing description confirms includes fine-tuning the ranking model shared across all customers. An opt-out exists but is off by default. *(Questions B1, H3)*

- **Risk:** Applicant personal data leaves Northwind's control through model weights, with no practical way to delete it. Potential conflict with GDPR purpose limitation for EU applicants.
- **Remediation:** Enable the opt-out before any data is loaded; replace it with a contractual no-training commitment.

### Finding 2 · Unmanaged dependency on an upstream foundation model (High)
Interview summaries are generated by a third-party large language model via API. The model provider is not on Orrery's sub-processor list, Orrery does not pin model versions, and it has no process to re-validate outputs or notify customers when the upstream model changes. *(C1, C2, C3)*

- **Risk:** Summary content and quality can change without notice, and Northwind's applicant data reaches a fourth party it has never assessed.
- **Remediation:** Add the model provider to the sub-processor list; require advance notice of upstream model changes with re-validation results.

### Finding 3 · Bias testing was one-time and US-only (High)
Orrery tested ranking outcomes for adverse impact by sex and race/ethnicity before launch in 2024, using US applicant data. It has not repeated the test, does not monitor outcomes in production, and has not evaluated performance for EU applicants or non-native English speakers. *(D2, D3, G1)*

- **Risk:** Rankings may systematically disadvantage protected groups in Northwind's actual applicant pool, particularly EU applicants interviewed in a second language.
- **Remediation:** Bias testing on a population representative of Northwind's applicants before EU go-live, then at least annually; quarterly outcome monitoring by Northwind HR analytics.

### Finding 4 · Automated rejection without human review (High)
Auto-reject is enabled by default. Orrery cannot show which candidates were rejected without a recruiter ever viewing their profile. *(F2, G4)*

- **Risk:** Consequential decisions about individuals are made without meaningful human oversight. This is inconsistent with Northwind's duties as a deployer of a high-risk AI system and with GDPR Article 22 restrictions on solely automated decisions.
- **Remediation:** Disable auto-reject; require recruiter review of every rejection; obtain decision-level logs.

### Finding 5 · Optional "engagement signals" add-on infers emotion (Critical)
An optional add-on scores candidate "enthusiasm" and "confidence" from facial expression and vocal tone. Orrery's own regulatory assessment did not flag it. *(A4)*

- **Risk:** Emotion recognition in the workplace, including recruitment, is a prohibited practice under EU AI Act Article 5(1)(f), applicable since 2 February 2025.
- **Remediation:** Exclude the add-on from Northwind's order form globally, not just for EU roles.

### Strengths
- Named Head of Responsible AI with authority to block releases; AI policy approved by Orrery's board (A1, A2)
- Prompt injection testing and red-teaming before major releases (E1, E3)
- Model card and instructions for use published for the ranking model (F1)

## 5. Recommendation

**Do not approve for production.** A limited pilot for US roles may proceed once Findings 1, 4 and 5 are closed. EU deployment is conditional on closing Finding 3 and on the deployer controls in [Project 02](../02-eu-ai-act-classification/).

## 7. Contract clauses requested

*Illustrative language for discussion with counsel.*

1. **No training.** Customer Data, including inputs and outputs, shall not be used to train or fine-tune any model without Customer's prior written opt-in.
2. **Model change notice.** Supplier shall give 30 days' notice of any material change to models used in the Service, including upstream model versions, together with re-validation results.
3. **Fairness evidence.** Supplier shall provide bias testing results at least annually, including on data representative of Customer's applicant population where lawfully available.
4. **Deployer support.** Supplier shall provide instructions for use, decision-level logs exportable for at least six months, and reasonable cooperation with Customer's obligations under applicable AI regulation.
5. **AI incidents.** Supplier shall notify Customer within 72 hours of becoming aware that the Service produced materially erroneous, biased or harmful outputs affecting Customer.
6. **Prohibited features.** No emotion-inference functionality shall be enabled for Customer.
