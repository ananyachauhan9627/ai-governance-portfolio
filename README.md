## The gap this portfolio works in

Most organizations will govern AI they *buy*, not AI they build. Yet most AI governance guidance is written for model developers, and most third-party risk programs were designed before vendors shipped models. An AI vendor can pass a traditional security assessment cleanly while its biggest risks go unexamined: training on customer data, unannounced model changes, untested bias.

The first two projects follow one fictional scenario end to end: **Northwind Logistics**, a US company with EU subsidiaries, evaluating **Orrery Talent**, an AI candidate-screening vendor.
